# @wantsandneedsbrand — Style Fingerprint

Rolled up across 3 videos. See `videos/*/analysis.md` for per-video detail. Last distilled: 2026-10-17.

## Signature moves (consistent across videos)
- Hook pattern: an absurd or impressive visual in the first second (costume gag, huge stack of clothes, car stuck in traffic) that reads with the sound off
- Story shape: concept/skit first, product as the payoff — never opens on the product
- Caption treatment: plain white TikTok-style sans, small, no box; either one line per beat or one fixed POV line; 2–3 word chunks when there is voiceover
- Transition rotation: hard cuts almost everywhere, one standout moment per video (vignette wipe, throwing the clothes off the roof, the flying-car VFX)
- Face treatment: mostly full-body, tripod or handheld, natural daylight; faces matter less than the fit
- Palette: daylight blue sky / concrete grey + black and charcoal product, white text
- Energy profile: build-up → one big payoff → calm product shots → end card

## What to steal (ranked by Hyperframes copy-ability)
1. Drop/restock end card: brand mark + product name + date or "NOW LIVE" in bold outlined serif over a flat-lay — easy kinetic-type outro
2. One-line captions at a fixed position for the skit section (`hyperframes` captions reference)
3. Product section structure: full body → medium → close-up of details → flat-lay, ending on "link in bio"
4. Flash-forward or "pattern break" right before the product appears
5. The skit/VFX idea itself — the user films or sources it; the build adds captions, timing and the end card

## Don't copy
- Their crown logo, script wordmark, "OG BLACK SET" naming, exact joke lines and stunts
